QUICK INSTRUCTIONS

Using CareNote

A short guide for users and project reviewers.

03 / 03
  1. 01
    Choose symptoms carefully

    Select only what you have experienced. Add duration, severity, progression, and notes in your own words.

  2. 02
    Read the label as a dataset match

    The predicted label is not a diagnosis, likelihood, triage result, or recommendation.

  3. 03
    Use the report to prepare

    Review the summary and discuss relevant symptoms, measurements, and health context with a clinician.

  4. 04
    Get urgent help when needed

    Do not wait for this app if symptoms are severe, sudden, worsening, or concerning.

SAFETY BOUNDARIES

What this project does not do

Keep these limits visible when reviewing outputs.

!
No diagnosis or treatment

CareNote is not a medical device and does not recommend treatment or medication changes.

!
No calibrated confidence

A label is a classifier output, not a probability that someone has a disease.

!
Warning checklist is incomplete

A negative warning-sign screen cannot rule out an emergency or replace clinical assessment.

!
Dataset bias and provenance matter

Source quality, representativeness, consent, and licensing need review before broader use.
