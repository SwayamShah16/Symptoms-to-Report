Your summary, step by step

  1. You choose symptoms and optionally add duration, self-reported severity, and notes.
  2. The system compares the selected symptoms with patterns in its reference dataset.
  3. A separate safety checklist checks for a limited set of warning signs.
  4. The information is arranged into a readable summary that you can download as a PDF.

About the reference information

The reference categories and descriptions come from a structured symptom dataset. The displayed pattern is one possible match within that dataset, based on the symptoms selected.

Symptoms can have many causes, and this summary does not consider every aspect of an individual's health. A qualified healthcare professional can interpret symptoms alongside medical history, examination, and any necessary tests.

Important health information

CareNote is intended to help organize information for a healthcare conversation. It does not provide a diagnosis, rule out a condition, or recommend treatment. The warning-sign checklist is limited; not seeing a warning does not mean an emergency is ruled out. If symptoms are sudden, severe, or worsening, seek urgent medical help rather than waiting for a result.

Privacy

Form submissions are processed to create the current summary and are not intentionally saved as report history. Avoid entering names, addresses, identification numbers, or other details that directly identify you. Downloaded PDFs may contain sensitive information, so keep them private.
