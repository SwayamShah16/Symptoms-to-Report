  1. Check for emergencies

    You are asked about emergency warning signs first. If you tick one, emergency advice appears immediately.

  2. Describe your symptoms

    Use Smart Fill or the list. Say how long, how severe, and whether they are getting worse.

  3. Get your report

    See a plain-language summary, questions for your healthcare professional, and guidance. Print it or save it as a PDF.

Safety and privacy

What this tool does

  • Organises what you tell it into a readable report.
  • Suggests questions you may want to ask.
  • Gives general guidance on how soon to seek care, using fixed, visible rules.

What it does not do

  • Diagnose any condition or suggest medicines.
  • Replace a doctor, nurse or emergency service.
  • Rule out an emergency. A calm result never means you are safe.

How it works inside

The safety check is a set of hand-written if-then rules. Smart Fill uses everyday-language pattern matching. Neither is machine learning, and neither has been clinically validated. The only generative AI is the optional wording helper.

Your privacy

Everything runs in your browser. Nothing is saved or sent anywhere, and closing the tab clears your answers. If the optional AI checkbox appears and you tick it, your answers (without a name) are sent to the AI provider. Printing or downloading a report creates a file on your device, so keep it private.

      Emergency
      Get emergency help now. Do not wait for anything else.
      Urgent
      Speak to a healthcare professional today.
      Soon
      Arrange an appointment in the next few days.
      Monitor
      No rule fired. Keep watching your symptoms. This never rules out a problem.
